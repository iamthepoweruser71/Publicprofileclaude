Cookie Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how Kumar Abhinav's portfolio website ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website. It explains what these technologies are and why we use them, as well as your rights to control our use of them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, Kumar Abhinav) are called "first-party cookies". Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies". Third-party cookies enable features or functionality to be provided on or through the website (e.g., analytics, interactive content, and advertising).

Always Active - These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems.

These cookies are essential for you to browse the website and use its features, such as accessing secure areas. Without these cookies, services you have asked for cannot be provided.

Why Do We Use Cookies?

We use first-party and third-party cookies for several reasons:

  • Essential Operations: Some cookies are required for technical reasons for our website to operate properly
  • Analytics: To understand how visitors use our website and improve user experience
  • Security: To protect against malicious activity and ensure secure access
  • Preferences: To remember your settings and preferences
  • Marketing: To deliver relevant content and measure campaign effectiveness

How Can You Control Cookies?

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by clicking on the appropriate opt-out links in our Cookie Preference Center that appears when you first visit our website.

Cookie Preference Center

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in our website footer. This will display the Cookie Preference Center where you can:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject all non-essential cookies
  • Select specific cookie categories to accept

Browser Controls

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. However, if you limit the ability of websites to set cookies, you may worsen your overall user experience.

Note: If you delete all cookies, you will lose any preferences you have set and will need to set them again when you next visit our website.

Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to access: You can request copies of your personal data
  • Right to rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure: You can request deletion of your data
  • Right to restrict processing: You can request limitation on data processing
  • Right to data portability: You can request transfer of your data
  • Right to object: You can object to processing of your data

Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature that lets you tell websites that you do not want to have your online activities tracked. Currently, we do not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals.
